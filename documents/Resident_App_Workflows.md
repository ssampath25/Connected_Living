# Resident App Workflows

## 1. Visitor Management

### Invite a Guest
1.  Navigate to the **Visitors** page via the bottom navigation or Dashboard quick action.
2.  Tap on the **+ (Plus)** button or "Invite Visitor".
3.  Select **Guest** tab.
4.  Enter the Guest's **Name**, **Phone Number** (Optional), and **Date/Time** of visit.
5.  Click **Generate Code**.
6.  Share the generated **Visitor Code** or **QR Code** via WhatsApp/SMS.

### Pre-Approve Delivery
1.  Navigate to **Visitors > Invite**.
2.  Select **Delivery** tab.
3.  Choose the **Vendor** (e.g., Amazon, Zomato, Swiggy).
4.  Select **Today** or a future date.
5.  Tap **Notify Security**. 
    *Result: Security guard sees the approval on their device and allows entry without calling.*

### Edit Frequent Visitor Permissions
1.  On the **Visitors** page, scroll to the **Saved Visitors** section.
2.  Tap on the visitor's profile (e.g., "Maid").
3.  Review their details.
4.  Modify their **Allowed Time Slots** (e.g., Change from "Morning" to "All Day").
5.  Tap **Save Changes**.

### Revoke a Visitor Pass
1.  Navigate to **Visitors > Saved/Frequent Visitors**.
2.  Tap on the visitor profile you wish to deactivate.
3.  Scroll to the bottom and tap **Revoke Pass**.
4.  Confirm the action in the security dialog.
    *Result: The visitor is removed from the active list and security is notified.*

## 2. Financials

### Pay Maintenance Bill
1.  Go to the **Payments** tab.
2.  Locate the bill under **Pending Dues**.
3.  Tap **Pay Now**.
4.  Review the Amount Breakdown.
5.  Select Payment Method (UPI, Card, Netbanking - *Simulation*).
6.  Confirm Payment.
7.  *Success*: The bill moves to the **History** tab and a receipt is generated.

### Secure Profile Update (Cross-Channel OTP)
1.  Go to **Profile > Personal Details**.
2.  Change a sensitive field (e.g., **Phone Number**).
3.  An **Email OTP** modal will appear (Security Focus Mode).
    *Note: Navigation is disabled until verification is complete.*
4.  Enter the OTP received on your **Email**.
5.  Tap **Verify & Update**.

## 3. Booking & Services

### Book an Amenity
1.  Go to **Services/Amenities** section.
2.  Select an amenity (e.g., **Tennis Court**).
3.  Tap **Book Slot**.
4.  Select a **Date**.
5.  Choose an available **Time Slot**.
6.  Confirm Booking.
    *Result: Slot is reserved and appears in "My Bookings".*

### Raise a Complaint
1.  Go to **Settings/More > Help & Support**.
2.  Tap **Raise Ticket** / **Contact Admin**.
3.  Select Category (e.g., **Plumbing**).
4.  Describe the issue (e.g., "Leaking tap in master bathroom").
5.  Select a **Preferred Date** (Calendar) and **Preferred Time** (Clock).
    *Note: Both are mandatory for specific assistance categories.*
6.  Attach a photo of the issue (Optional, but recommended).
7.  Submit.
    *Result: Ticket ID generated. Check progress with detailed "Preferred Slot" in the Support page.*

## 4. Vehicle Management

### Add a New Vehicle
1.  Go to **Profile > My Vehicles**.
2.  Tap **Add Vehicle**.
3.  Enter **Vehicle Number**, **Model**, and **Type** (Car/Bike).
4.  Submit request.
    *Result: Status shows "Pending Approval" until Admin validates.*
